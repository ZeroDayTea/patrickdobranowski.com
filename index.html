About Resume Writing

I’m a student at Vanderbilt University, studying mathematics, physics, and computer science as an Ingram Scholar.

Interests

My academic and professional interests span quantum optics and quantum electrodynamics, pure mathematics — particularly group theory and representation theory — and machine learning. I also enjoy programming and topics in systems, robotics, low-latency engineering, type theory, and security.

Research

My research focus is quantum models of high harmonic generation and simulation software, as well as graph theory applied to real-time systems algorithms. Prior to that, I worked on privacy-preserving machine learning, developing techniques for detecting deepfake audio content.

Experience

I have previously worked at SpaceX, Trail of Bits (twice), the ELI-ALPS Laser Research Institute, and Vanderbilt’s Institute for Software Integrated Systems. I was also a co-founder at Veria Labs, running security audits and automated security projects.

Elsewhere
